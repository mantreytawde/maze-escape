Maze Escape

You come to in a concrete maze. Three padlocked gates stand between you and the door out, and each key hangs at the end of a dead end somewhere before its gate.

Stop at any crossing and a painted arrow shows the way to whatever you need next. Expect at least five minutes.

Headset

  • Right stick forward aim a teleport, release to jump
  • Right stick left / right turn
  • Left stick walk
  • Grip take a key, again to drop it
  • Touch key to padlock unlock
  • A or X ask for a trail

Keyboard

  • W A S D walk
  • Mouse look
  • E take a key, unlock a gate
  • H ask for a trail
  • Esc pause
