How to play

Find three keys, open three gates, get out

  • Keys go into your pocket when you walk up to them. Walk up to the matching gate and it unlocks by itself.
  • Questions pop up every half minute. Answer right and arrows on the walls show you the way for a while. Answer wrong and you get nothing until the next one.
  • Hearts. You have three. The zombie hunts the wrong turnings, and each catch costs one.
  • Danger zones turn the lights red. Follow the arrows out before the timer runs out, or it finds you.
  • Flickering lights make it hard to see. Keep moving.

Hands (Quest)

  • Open hand straight out walk forward
  • Open hand back by shoulder walk back
  • Arm out to the side turn that way
  • Right pinch, aim, let go teleport
  • Say the answer or point and pinch
  • Left palm to face, pinch menu

Controllers

  • Left stick walk
  • Right stick left / right turn
  • Right stick forward aim a teleport, release to jump
  • Trigger press buttons
  • A, B, X or Y menu

Keyboard

  • W A S D walk
  • Mouse look
  • 1 2 3 or click to answer
  • E unlock a gate
  • Esc pause menu
